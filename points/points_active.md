## #1595 (28 Nov 2026) -- BYTE AND HALFWORD LOADS (lb lh lbu lhu) IN sc_sys_v26 / uc_core_v2w, UNFUSED TASKS; PASS IN THE POOL TEST AT S 4 AND S 8
