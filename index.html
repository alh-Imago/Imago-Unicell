FIG. 01 WIRED-OR BUS — TWO-ARRIVAL FIRING

Topology is computation.

Imago UniCell is an FPGA-based computing architecture built from NOR-universal cells that communicate over a wired-OR bus. There is no global clock. Instead, causality emerges from wire delay itself — a cell fires only once signals arrive from two directions, so the physical layout of the wiring is the logic, not just the carrier for it.

The design has been validated on real Arria 10 GX660 silicon: cardinal bridges, wired-OR bus behaviour, transit cells, and a full inter-cell-message round trip all confirmed on physical hardware, not just in simulation.
