## What I got wrong today

### iCEBreaker Verilog session was unnecessary
PLAN.md had "a_preload_en / a_preload_val as cmd_latch bits" as a todo.
When I actually read unicell.v, preload_sel and shift bits were already
in silicon as cmd_bus transient modifiers. I'd added those items to PLAN.md
without checking the Verilog first.

Key correction from Alan: preload_sel is a cmd_BUS feature (transient,
per-transaction), not a cmd_LATCH feature (persistent cell state).
Conflating the two is the confusion that needs the naming convention.

### cmd_latch vs cmd_bus confusion
I briefly proposed adding a_preload_en as cmd_latch bits before Alan
corrected the distinction. The cmd_latch is the cell's persistent state
loaded by CMD_RECONFIGURE. cmd_bus modifiers are transient. These are
different things that must not be confused in code or docs.
