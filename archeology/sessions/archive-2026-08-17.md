The chain's exact bit format was then confirmed directly against the

`PROG_ID` pattern with no new mechanism needed. A real, hard
constraint found: these controls are non-functional in the highest-
precision (`m27x27`) mode — closed with an honest scope note that the
current substrate has no signed-number or fixed-point convention at

no signed representation at all. Added to both the design note and,
